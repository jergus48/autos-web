      Why an agent

      How is this different from ChatGPT?

      ChatGPT answers when you ask it. An agent does the work on its own, connected to your systems and following your rules.

      Connected to your systems

      It reads your mailbox, checks prices and stock in your catalog and customer history in your CRM, and writes to your Excel, Google Sheets or ERP.

      Works on its own, around the clock

      An email came in overnight or at the weekend? By the time you open your inbox, the order is logged and the reply is drafted.

      You stay in charge

      Replies go out only once you approve them, or automatically if you decide so. The agent flags anything unclear, and you can see everything it did.
