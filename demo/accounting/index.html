        Why an agent

        What changes for an accounting firm

        Most of the month-end work is the same few checks on a pile of documents, and most client questions have an answer in the books. The agents do that work and you look at the exceptions.

        Every document is checked

        VAT numbers, totals, duplicates and periods are checked on every document, so you only look at what fails.

        Clients get answers at once

        Questions about status, missing documents and VAT are answered from the books in seconds, and anything unclear reaches you with a summary.

        You stay in charge

        Nothing that fails a check is posted, tax questions go to you, and unusual documents such as contracts are flagged.

        In your firm

        What the real agents connect to

        In the demos everything is simulated. In your firm the agents work with the tools you already use.

        Your clients’ inbox or folder

        Collects documents from a shared mailbox, a folder or a client upload page.

        Your accounting software

        Prepares the journal in the format your software imports, so nothing is typed twice.

        Your chart of accounts

        Sorts every document into your categories and learns from your corrections.

        Your client channel

        Answers on your website chat, by email or in the messenger your clients already use.
