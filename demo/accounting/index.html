      ✓What the agent checks
      • VAT number: present and in a valid format. A receipt without one cannot be deducted.
      • Totals: net plus VAT must equal the total on the document.
      • Duplicates: same supplier, number and total as an earlier document.
      • Period: the date must fall in March 2026.
      • Foreign currency and reverse charge: converted, and flagged for you to confirm.
      • Contracts: never posted by the agent, always flagged for you.

      Why an agent

      What changes for an accounting firm

      Most of the month-end work is the same few checks on a pile of documents. The agent does the checks and you look at the exceptions.

      Every document is checked

      VAT numbers, totals, duplicates and periods are checked on every document, so you only look at what fails.

      One request per client

      Everything missing for the month is collected into a single message instead of ten separate emails.

      You stay in charge

      Nothing that fails a check is posted, and unusual documents such as contracts are flagged for you.

      In your firm

      What the real agent connects to

      In the demo everything is simulated. In your firm the agent works with the tools you already use.

      Your clients’ inbox or folder

      Collects documents from a shared mailbox, a folder or a client upload page.

      Your accounting software

      Prepares the journal in the format your software imports, so nothing is typed twice.

      Your chart of accounts

      Sorts every document into your categories and learns from your corrections.

      Your deadlines

      Reminds clients before the deadline, so the month closes on time.
