      Why an agent

      What changes for a construction company

      Enquiries arrive at all hours and most of them need the same first steps. The agent does those steps and you keep the decisions.

      Answers in minutes, day and night

      A customer who writes at 22:00 gets a reply before a competitor has opened their inbox.

      Prices from your own price list

      No guessing: every number comes from your prices and your rules, and you can change them at any time.

      You stay in charge

      Nothing goes out until you approve it. Big or unusual jobs get a site visit instead of a price by email.
