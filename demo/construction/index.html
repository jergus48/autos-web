          Why an agent

          What changes for a construction company

          Enquiries and site messages arrive at all hours, and most of them need the same first steps. The agents do those steps and you keep the decisions.

          Answers in minutes, day and night

          A customer who writes at 22:00 gets a reply before a competitor has opened their inbox.

          Numbers from your own data

          Prices come from your price list, dates from your plan and rules from you. No guessing, and you can change every one of them.

          You stay in charge

          Nothing goes out until you approve it. Big or unusual jobs get a site visit, and delays reach you as an alert.

          In your company

          What the real agents connect to

          In the demos everything is simulated. In your company the agents work with the tools you already use.

          Your mailbox

          Reads enquiries from Gmail or Outlook, also with photos and attachments.

          Your site chats

          Reads the daily messages from foremen in WhatsApp or Teams, also voice notes.

          Your prices and plans

          Uses your Excel, Google Sheets or estimating software, so prices and schedules are always yours.

          Your calendar and CRM

          Offers real free slots for site visits and logs every enquiry with its status and next step.
