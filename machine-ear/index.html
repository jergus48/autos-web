    How it works

    Three steps, no changes to your machine

    The sensor only listens. Nothing is wired into your machine or its controls.

    Place the sensor

    A small box with a microphone goes next to the machine. We set it up on site.

    It listens for a week

    The AI learns how your machine sounds when it starts, runs under load and idles, and which noises around it are normal.

    It warns you

    When the sound drifts away from normal, you get a message on your phone, by email or in Teams, saying what it heard.

    What it hears

    Faults that change the sound first

    Many failures start with a sound long before anything stops.

    Bearings and gears

    A worn bearing whines or grinds long before it seizes.

    Leaks

    Air, steam and gas leaks hiss at frequencies people rarely notice.

    Loose parts

    Belts, fans and guards start to knock or rattle.

    Pumps and compressors

    Cavitation, dry running and valve trouble change the sound.

    What it ignores

    People talking, doors, forklifts and radios are learned as background, so they do not set off alerts.

    Not every fault makes a sound. Before we install anything, we listen to your machine and tell you honestly whether this will work for it.

    What you get

    Hardware and software, from one team

    The sensor

    A small box with an industrial microphone. A vibration sensor can be added for machines that need it.

    The software

    Learning, detection and a simple page that shows every machine as normal or needs attention.

    Alerts

    On your phone, by email or in Teams, with a short note on what it heard and since when.

    Setup and support

    We install it, tune it to your machine and keep it working.

    Who it is for

    Workshops and factories with pumps, compressors, fans, conveyors, CNC machines and production lines. Anywhere a breakdown costs more than a sensor.

    Built to listen to machines, not people

    Raw audio is analysed on the device and is not stored. Only numbers that describe the sound leave it.
