About Swiftrix

A small studio that takes repetitive work off people’s desks

Swiftrix is an independent software automation studio in Vilnius, run by Ignas Lūža. We work for businesses where people lose hours to emails, orders, invoices and reports.

Automations and integrations

We connect the tools a company already uses, so data moves between them without copying by hand.

The role

How the work goes

You do the talking, we do the building. You make the calls yourself, we do not join them.

1

Call companies

Open the sales tool, pick a company, read its summary and phone the person who decides, for example the manager or owner.

2

Hold the audit call

A short conversation to find out which repetitive work the company could automate. Slides prepared for each company help you present, and no technical knowledge is needed. You log the result in the tool.

3

We take it from here

Within 5 business days Swiftrix prepares the proposal. If the company agrees, we sign the contract and build the solution. You can stay in the conversation, but you do not have to.

4

You get paid

Once the client has paid, you receive 35% of the project fee.

What you earn

35% of every project you bring in

35%of the project fee, excluding VAT, for every client you bring in.
  • Projects usually cost €1,500⁠–⁠4,000, so one client is worth about €525⁠–⁠1,400 to you.
  • You are paid after the client pays. Every month we send a statement, you send us an invoice, and we pay within 10 days.
  • A company you hold an audit call with stays registered to you for 6 months.
  • Commission only: there is no salary and no guaranteed income. What you earn depends on the calls you make.

Good to know

The terms in plain words

  • You are an independent partner, not an employee. You choose your own hours and place of work.
  • You need your own business status: self-employment, a business licence or a company, depending on your country. You calculate and pay your own taxes.
  • The agreement is not exclusive, and either side can end it with 14 days’ notice.
  • You present Swiftrix honestly. You may quote the usual price range, but you do not promise discounts, deadlines or results. Swiftrix signs the contracts with clients.
  • You call in the language of the company: Lithuanian in Lithuania, German in Germany, Austria and Switzerland.
  • You respect a “no”. If someone asks not to be contacted, you stop calling and tell us.

All terms are written down in a short agreement that we send you before you start.
