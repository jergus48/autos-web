What Swiftrix 3D does for your business

Reads a floor plan

Upload a plan as an image or PDF. The program finds the rooms, walls, doors and windows and works out the real measurements.

Furnishes it and checks it

It proposes three arrangements per room and measures walking routes, clear space and distances, so the layout is practical, not just pretty.

Furniture from a link

Paste an IKEA or other shop link. The program reads the size from the page, builds a 3D model from the product pictures and fits the room around it.

3D for your customers

Interactive 3D, top views, eye-level views and HD pictures, ready for your customers, your listings or your website.
