Document and protocol signing

Digital protocol signing on the factory floor

For: hakom.sk, aluprint.sk

The task

Signing off protocols on the factory floor was done on paper, and it added up to hundreds of paper forms.

What we built

A sign-off system for the factory floor. Workers log in with their factory ID card, see every new document they must review, and sign it digitally.

What it does now

The paper forms are replaced by a fully auditable record. At Aluprint the same card-based system runs across the production line, so management can see at a glance that every worker has read and accepted the latest protocols, with no paperwork to chase.

Invoice archive cleanup

Knowing which paper invoices can be thrown away

For: gaya.sk

The task

A large archive of paper invoices had to be sorted out: which of them could be discarded?

What we built

A program wired into their scanner. It checks each scanned paper invoice against the database.

What it does now

Staff are told exactly which paper invoices can be discarded, which frees up a large amount of storage space.
