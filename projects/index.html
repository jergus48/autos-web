Content creation and creator portal

A content platform that creates, approves and publishes on its own

For: joinupshift.com

The task

Content for social networks was made by hand: finding images, writing texts, putting slides together and posting on each channel one by one. Creators also had to be recruited, onboarded and paid.

What we built

A fully automated flow. The system generates the images and texts, assembles the slides and videos, sends them for approval and publishes straight to YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. The same platform runs a creator portal for recruiting and onboarding clippers and managing their accounts. It checks views and applies tiered payout rules automatically.

What it does now

Content goes from start to published post without manual steps, and one dashboard tracks the sales, views and revenue each clipper generates. For creators this saves €1,000 to €2,000 a month.

  • €1,000 to €2,000saved per month for creators
  • A to Zimages, texts and publishing
Document scanning console

A console that sorts a whole batch of scanned documents

For: gaya.sk

The task

Employees compared documents with the database by hand, page by page, to decide what to keep and which paper invoices could be thrown away.

What we built

A program wired into their scanner, with a console on top of it. It sorts the whole batch into keep, reject, error and duplicate, and shows only the exceptions that deserve attention.

What it does now

The whole batch is sorted at once. Staff are told exactly which paper invoices can be discarded, which frees up a large amount of storage space.

  • One clickto scan and sort
  • Whole batchsorted at once

Quality document management

Digital protocols and read receipts for two factories

For: hakom.sk, aluprint.sk

The task

Instructions and procedures were distributed on paper, with no proof of who had read what. Signing off protocols produced hundreds of paper forms.

What we built

One system for two companies. Workers log in with their factory ID card, and every employee sees and confirms the documents that apply to them. The system keeps the full version and revision history.

What it does now

Proof of reading is ready for an audit. At Aluprint, management can see at a glance that every worker has read and accepted the latest protocols, with no paperwork to chase.

  • Audit readyproof of who read what
  • 2 in 1one shared platform
